---
name: Preliminary pattern safety
description: The non-negotiable validation status and physical sampling gate for generated JNX pattern outputs.
---

All generated pattern, grading, tech-pack, DXF, SVG, manifest, and factory-package outputs must remain clearly marked `PRELIMINARY_UNVALIDATED`. Never describe them as approved for bulk cutting.

**Why:** Parametric development patterns and a global 10 mm allowance cannot replace fabric-specific review, physical fitting, edge-by-edge seam allowance decisions, and a signed PP sample.

**How to apply:** Preserve the warning in the interface and exported files. Any future approval workflow must require qualified pattern-maker review, physical sampling, correction, and explicit PP-sample signoff rather than a simple status toggle.
